I’ve always wanted to create a small blog, a place where I could write posts and, years from now, look back and understand a little more about who I was at different points in my life. I’ve written countless drafts over the years, but somehow, I never actually published any of them.

As a developer, I’ve always loved building things. At the same time, I often find myself stuck on the what: what should I build, what should I write about, and whether an idea is actually worth sharing.

So, I decided to take a different approach. I built a small framework that gives me a place to put my ideas—whether they’re fresh or old, good or bad, shallow or deep. There’s no particular goal other than to create, experiment, and hopefully learn something along the way.

Always trying to be creative. Always trying to be inventive.
